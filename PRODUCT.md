# BouquetBrowse

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-approved: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, CSS Modules; FastAPI, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy, Alembic; PostgreSQL with pgvector; Supabase Auth and Storage; Docker and GitHub Actions.

## Users
Hanoi flower buyers comparing bouquets on mobile, invited research testers, and catalog administrators.

## Product Purpose
Turn permitted social advertisements into an attributable, searchable flower catalog. Help customers find alternatives and compare sufficiently equivalent advertised configurations before contacting the original seller.

## Positioning
Separate visual recommendations from evidence-qualified, seller-balanced price comparisons.

## Operating Context
Private research pilot, Vietnamese-first with English support, VND, 100–1,000 initial listings, solo developer, $100–200 monthly budget. Public-post-first acquisition with operator imports when capabilities are unavailable.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Implement the approved MVP end to end, with labeled demonstration records and capability-gated live integrations. Preserve source text, evidence, uncertainty, commercial observation times, and review history. Do not invent inventory, prices, stem counts, guarantees, historical market data, or unavailable API capabilities.

## Brand Commitments
BouquetBrowse. Preserve the original tagline: finding your flowers. Restrained botanical accents, quiet mobile layout, readable Vietnamese typography, photography foregrounded.

## Evidence on Hand
README and approved technical blueprint. No authenticated Meta access, real seller dataset, trained flower model, or validated model performance is established. Demonstration sellers and their advertisements are fictional.

## Product Principles
- Source evidence before commercial claims.
- Unknown is an accepted result.
- Similarity is not equivalence.
- One seller, one representative in price benchmarks.
- Make the first implementation runnable across the full MVP.

## Threads discovery and digest update — 29 October 2026

The approved implementation now includes bounded, account-authenticated Threads browsing on the operator's awake Windows computer: search, FYP, profiles, posts and replies. Instagram browser automation and cloud-hosted browsing remain deferred. Capture conversation evidence independently of seller/catalog identity; buyers and unresolved accounts never automatically become sellers. Stable offer occurrences survive recognition corrections. Seller-rooted offers, seller replies to buyer requests and explicitly labeled recreation quotations are distinct evidence patterns.

After the twenty-conversation workflow proof and 100-image mixed-bouquet benchmark, automatically publish evidence-supported provisional offers. Commercial review remains required for benchmark eligibility, not every catalog publication. Composition is florist-level and multi-label, with separate evidence/uncertainty per type. Daily in-app digests summarize attributable changes. See docs/THREADS-DISCOVERY.md for execution boundaries, operating limits and delivery gates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard access, visible focus, accessible names, semantic controls, reduced motion, responsive mobile comparisons, Vietnamese diacritics and localization.
